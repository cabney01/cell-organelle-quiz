Can You Explain the Process?

Say this out loud before you finish:

DNA is transcribed into mRNA in the nucleus. The mRNA leaves the nucleus and goes to a ribosome. The ribosome reads the mRNA codons, tRNA brings amino acids, and the amino acids are joined to build a protein.
