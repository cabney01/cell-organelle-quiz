Teacher Explanation

DNA contains the instructions for making a protein. During transcription, which happens in the nucleus, a section of DNA is used to make an mRNA copy.

The mRNA leaves the nucleus and travels to a ribosome in the cytoplasm. During translation, the ribosome reads the mRNA three bases at a time as codons.

tRNA brings the correct amino acids for each codon. The amino acids are joined together to form a protein.
